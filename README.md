It'a an idle game about providing food for your cubs. In the game you have to gather food while your cubs are hungry and you have to not let them starve.

In the game you will have 2 game modes to chose from, "Cozy" where your cubs can not die and you just have to feed them to make them happy. Or you could choose the "Survival" mode where you have to make sure your cubs don't starve, and you have to protect them against other predetors.
In the "Cozy" difficulty your cubs will be able to grow to adulthood and leave the cave, and sometimes maybe come back with a gift.
In "Survival" difficulty your cubs will not reach adulthood and will always need food from you if not they will starve and you will lose. You will get progressively more cubs and they will get progressively more hungry.
